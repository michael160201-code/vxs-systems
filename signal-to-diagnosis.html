VXS / PUBLIC METHOD NOTE 02

From signal to a bounded diagnostic decision.

A useful diagnostic process makes uncertainty visible. It does not turn a limited signal into a confident story.

The public method

VXS moves from operating evidence to a next validation decision through four deliberately limited steps.

01 / Frame

State the product system, operating stage, claimed outcome, and decision that is actually pending.

02 / Route

Map the available signal to the relevant T/B/C/M/S layers and identify interfaces that could change the outcome.

03 / Bound

Separate observations, reasonable inferences, and missing inputs. Do not convert a pattern into a conclusion without evidence.

04 / Validate

Specify the smallest appropriate next action: a test, measurement, document check, comparison, or controlled trial.

What VXS can explain publicly

General diagnostic concepts, abstract failure modes, method boundaries, and the conditions that make a validation decision necessary.

What remains private

Client submissions, raw records, exact formula ratios, named suppliers, and client-specific interpretations are not public method content.
